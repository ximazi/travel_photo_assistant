A detected headcount does not prove visual prominence. Gemini gave A full goal credit both times despite the subject being small and partially blended with the rocks (a distinct goal dimension for human comparison). Wording adjustment to test: clarify that a real photo showing visible people remains in scope even if no person dominates, so a failed user goal is recorded as a score, not an excuse to discard the pair. Ask separately for evidence of prominence versus counts. A true no-person, unreadable or non-photographic input should still abstain. This can improve measurement but will not resolve actual missed people. Do not tune weights based on these two pairs. Future evaluation needs independent human raters and more authors/scenes.
